Yubo Zhang (张玉波) is an Associate Professor at [Minjiang University](https://www.mju.edu.cn/), in the Minjiang Collaborative Center for Theoretical Physics, Fuzhou, China.

He studies complex magnetic materials from first principles, and he develops density-functional theory methods.

## Education

* Ph.D. in Optics, Department of Physics, Harbin Institute of Technology (2008–2012)
* M.Sc. in Optics, Department of Physics, Harbin Institute of Technology (2006–2008)
* B.Eng. in Optical Information Science and Technology, Shandong University (2002–2006)

## Appointments

* Associate Professor, Minjiang University (2022–present)
* Research Associate Professor, Southern University of Science and Technology (2018–2021)
* Postdoctoral researcher, University of Texas / Tulane University, with Jianwei Sun and John Perdew (2016–2018)
* Postdoctoral researcher, Temple University, with Xifan Wu (2014–2016)
* Postdoctoral researcher, Shanghai Institute of Ceramics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, with Wenqing Zhang (2012–2014)
